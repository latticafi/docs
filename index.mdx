## What is Lattica?

Lattica is a decentralized protocol that provides credit and leverage to prediction market traders. Currently, Lattica supports Polymarket, with Outcome and other venues soon to follow.

## Technical Overview

Leverage on prediction markets is a difficult challenge due to jump risk: the tendency of prediction market positions to “jump” between price levels. For instance, a 90c position can be worth 0 in half a second on the arrival of new information. That makes liquidating these positions extremely unreliable.

To defeat jump risk, Lattica built a calibrated risk engine, WARHORSE, which calculates a position's expected loss over the next 15 minutes. The expected loss is then charged to the trader as a premium to be paid before the leveraged position can be opened. A full discussion of the details behind WARHORSE can be found in our Whitepaper, [*Unlocking Liquidity*](https://lattica.finance/unlocking-liquidity.pdf).
