Credit and margin are fundamental pieces of the modern financial system. Roughly 85-90% of daily S&P 500 notional trades through levered products. Lattica’s goal is to bring the same financial primitives to prediction markets, allowing it to scale into a trillion-dollar industry.

Lattica currently offers three products:

1. Borrowing against current prediction market positions.
2. Leverage on new prediction market positions.
3. Liquidity provision for yield (recurring payments).

Although all three are slightly different products, the overall goal remains the same: improve market liquidity and the trader experience. Borrowing against current positions lets traders use capital that would otherwise sit idle, leverage provides more buying power, and liquidity provision lets people profit from prediction market growth without actively trading.

For you, the trader, the result is simple: Lattica gives you more money to trade prediction markets with.

Lattica is developed by Lattica, Inc., a Delaware C-Corp that has raised funding from Polychain, Contribution Capital, Jsqaure, and V3V Ventures. Our core tenets are:

1. **Transparency**: You deserve to know how your money is being used. As such, we open-source as much as possible and provide detailed [papers](https://lattica.finance/whitepapers) on our risk models and methodology.
2. **Safety**: User funds are sacrosanct. Security (link to security page?) is always at the top of our mind and audits are done regularly.
3. **Performance**: Our job is to help you make more money as a trader. Every design and development decision is done with this in mind, and user feedback is always quickly turned into improvements.
