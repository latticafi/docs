---
title: "How to borrow against a current position"
---

Borrowing against a current prediction market position allows you to receive USDC in return for staking your position as collateral.

1. Find the market you wish to borrow against on the Markets page (need link) or using the search button (magnifying glass in the top right corner).
2. On the trade terminal page, click the “Borrow” button directly above the order book.
3. If you are using the same DeFi wallet you use on Polymarket, your position will automatically be loaded.
4. If you created a new wallet or are using email, you can deposit the position by:
   1. Clicking “Deposit” in the top right corner.
   2. Not actually sure how to export positions out of Polymarket
5. Once you have your position ready, enter how much of the position you wish to borrow against.
6. Select your desired LTV. LTV is the size of the loan relative to the value of the collateral. The higher your LTV, the higher the liquidation price.
7. Select your desired loan length.
8. Lattica will then automatically return a quote for the size of the loan, the liquidation price of the loan (the price of the collateral at which Lattica has to sell your position), and the total fees.
9. Once you pay the upfront risk premium, USDC is automatically deposited into your wallet.
10. To retrieve your collateral, repay the loan anytime before the loan expires. If you fail to do so, Lattica will sell your collateral.
11. If at any point during the loan the value of the collateral drops to the liquidation price, Lattica will sell the collateral at the market rate.
