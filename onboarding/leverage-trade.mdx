---
title: "How to leverage trade"
---

Leverage trading increases your buying power, potentially massively increasing winnings, however, it also introduces the risk of liquidation.

1. Find the market you wish to leverage trade on the Markets page (need link) or using the search button (magnifying glass in the top right corner).
2. On the trade terminal page, enter your desired size. That acts as your margin, which is the number the leverage ultimately multiplies.
3. Slide the leverage slider to your desired leverage amount. For example, if your size is $1,000 and you choose 5x leverage, your position will be worth $5,000.
4. After your size and leverage are chosen, Lattica will return a liquidation price and total fee. The liquidation price is the price at which Lattica sells your position.
5. Once you pay the upfront risk premium, your position will be open for the next 15 minutes.
6. You can close your position at any point over the 15 minute term to receive a time-based rebate. For instance, if your premium was 0.01% and your position was open for 5 minutes, you will be rebated 0.0066%.
7. If you don’t close your position, the position will automatically roll-over at a new premium charge.
8. Premiums are taken from your overall account balance first, then your margin (the size in the position).
9. If at any point during the position lifespan the price hits the liquidation price, Lattica will sell your collateral. Any remaining margin after lenders are repaid, minus a liquidation fee, will be returned to your wallet.
