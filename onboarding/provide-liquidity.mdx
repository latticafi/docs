---
title: "How to provide liquidity"
---

The Lattica Liquidity Provider (LLP) allows lenders to earn a yield on Lattica’s trading activity.

1. Navigate to the “Earn” page.
2. Select the size in USDC that you wish to deposit.
3. Click “Deposit.”
4. Lattica will automatically credit your wallet with LatUSD.
5. As your USDC deposit grows from trader interest payments, liquidation fees, and unused premium charges, Lattica will continuously credit your wallet with a corresponding amount of LatUSD.
6. To withdraw your capital, click “Withdraw” and enter the amount of LatUSD you wish to redeem for USDC.
7. You may withdraw at any time, subject to available liquidity.

Note: Liquidity providing is currently only available to select partners. For more information, please email [contact@lattica.finance](mailto:contact@lattica.finance).
