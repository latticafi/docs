## What do I need to start trading on Lattica?

You can trade on Lattica with a normal DeFi wallet or by logging in with your email address.

If you choose to use a normal DeFi wallet, you need:

1. An EVM wallet
   - If you don’t already have an EVM wallet (e.g., Rabby, MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase Wallet), you can set one up easily at [https://rabby.io/](https://rabby.io/).
   - After downloading a wallet extension for your browser, create a new wallet.
   - Your wallet has a secret recovery phrase – anyone with access to your private key or seed phrase can access your funds. Do not share these with anyone. Best practice is to record these and store them in a safe physical location.
2. Collateral
   - USDC and POL (gas to deposit) on Polygon.

## How do I onboard to Lattica?

Lattica is offered through two venues:

1. (Need link for prod website) Web
2. (Ned link for app store download) iOS, Android

If you choose to log in to (prod website link) with email:

1. Click the "Connect" button and enter your email address. After you press "Submit," within a few seconds, a 6-digit code will be sent to your email. Type in the 6-digit code to log in.
2. Now you're connected. All that's left is to deposit. Click “Deposit” to open the deposit window:
   - You can send USDC on Polygon to the deposit address shown (or scan the QR code) from a centralized exchange or another DeFi wallet. Your deposit arrives as USDC on Polygon.
3. You’re now ready to trade.

If you choose to connect to (prod website link) with a DeFi wallet:

1. Click the “Connect” button and choose a wallet to connect. A pop-up will appear in your wallet extension asking you to connect to Polygon. Press “Connect.”
2. Click the “Enable Trading” button. A pop-up will appear in your wallet extension asking you to sign a gas-less transaction. Press "Sign."
3. Deposit to Polygon, choosing USDC on Polygon. Enter the amount you want to deposit and click “Deposit.” Confirm the transaction in your EVM wallet.
4. You're now ready to trade.

## How do I get USDC onto Lattica?

1. Many CEXs and bridges support withdrawals on Lattica.
2. You can also transfer USDC from other chains via CCTP. For this, you will need USDC and the native gas token on your source chain. The gas is only for depositing. Trading on Lattica is gas-free.
3. Polygon is a common chain to receive USDC, if your centralized exchange does not directly support Hyperliquid.
   - To get USDC on Polygon, you can use various bridges, such as [https://portal.polygon.technology/bridge](https://portal.polygon.technology/bridge), [https://app.debridge.finance/](https://app.debridge.finance/), [https://swap.mayan.finance/](https://swap.mayan.finance/), [https://app.across.to/bridge?](https://app.across.to/bridge?), [https://jumper.exchange/](https://jumper.exchange/), [https://synapseprotocol.com/](https://synapseprotocol.com/), and [https://relay.link/bridge](https://relay.link/bridge)
   - Alternatively, you can move funds directly to Polygon from a centralized exchange, if you’re already using one.
   - Once you have POL and USDC on Polygon, you can deposit by clicking the “Deposit” button on [https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/trade](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/trade)

## How do I withdraw USDC from Lattica?

1. On Lattica, click the “Withdraw” button in the bottom right.
2. Follow the steps. Depending on the withdrawal chain and method, there may be small gas fees to process the withdrawal.
