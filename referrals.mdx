---
title: "Referrals"
---

Referral codes can be created at (need link). Lattica has a three-tier referral system.

- Direct referrals earn you 35% of your origination fees.
- Second-degree referrals (users that are referred by someone you refer) earn you 3% of their origination fees.
- Third-degree referrals (users that are referred by second-degree referrals) earn you 1% of their origination fees.

Referral codes can be shared with a unique sign-up link (need link) or by giving your referee your code directly, which they can then input when they sign up.

Referral rewards continuously accrue and can be claimed once >$1. Claimed referral rewards are reflected in your spot balance.
