Lattica is developed by Lattica, Inc. Lattica is currently a team of six full-time employees and one contractor, led by [Stephen](https://x.com/SteveFlanders22) and [William](https://x.com/wkflanders) Flanders, brothers who both went to Haverford College and the University of Pennsylvania and who have been involved in prediction markets since 2023.

Other members of the team include Njoku Emmanuel (Founder of Ultramarkets and Lazerpay, engineer at MakerDAO, and Paradigm fellow), Ben Kling (BD Lead at Axiom and Bullpen), Pelumi Alesh (designer at Ultramarkets and Bloomberg Terminal), and Nicholas Zhu (UPenn).

The team is spread around the world but united by one mission: turning prediction markets into the biggest market on Earth, and the team has been funded by Polychain, Contribution Capital, V3V Ventures, and Jsqaure to do just that.

Everyone in Lattica is a veteran prediction market builder and trader with an intense desire to provide the best trading experience possible. We actively listen to and implement user feedback, and are always available on [X](https://x.com/lattica) or Email ([contact@lattica.finance](mailto:contact@lattica.finance)).
