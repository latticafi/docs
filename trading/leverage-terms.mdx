---
title: "Leverage Terms"
---

Leverage trades on Lattica operate in 15-minute “Terms.” This means that every 15 minutes, you will receive a fresh interest rate and WARHORSE premium. This is for risk management. The purpose of WARHORSE is to forecast the expected loss of positions. Because the market changes so often (the first quarter and fourth quarter of a game do not have the same level of risk, for example), we must continuously reprice the loan.

Terms automatically roll-over until either liquidation or trade closure.
