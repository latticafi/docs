---
title: "Margin"
---

Margin is the amount of equity you have in a given position. It determines two things: total position size at origination and liquidation price.

Margin is the number that leverage multiplies to determine total position size. For example, a trade with $1,000 of margin with 5x leverage will have a total position size of $5,000.

Margin also determines liquidation price. The more margin you have, the lower the liquidation price. For example, a $1,000 total position with $500 with margin will have a lower liquidation price than a $1,000 total position with $200 with margin. The reason for this is structural. In the event of a liquidation, $500 of margin gives more buffer before lender capital is impacted.

You can both add to and remove margin mid-Term. To do so, click the dropdown under “Open position” in the top left of the trade panel.
