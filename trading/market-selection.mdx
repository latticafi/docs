---
title: "Market selection"
---

We believe traders are best served and most profitable when they are able to trade the markets they have an edge in. As such, Lattica offers every market on Polymarket except for mention markets and crypto up/down markets.

Risk in a market is handled by WARHORSE. Riskier markets and positions have higher premiums. It is up to the trader, not Lattica, whether they are willing to pay the risk premium.
