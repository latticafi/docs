---
title: "Order types"
---

Lattica currently offers two types of orders: market and price trigger.

Market orders are executed at the current market price, dependent on slippage.

Price trigger orders are executed at a trader’s selected price, dependent on slippage. When the price of the market hits the trigger price, the trade is market ordered. For example, if the trigger price is 40 cents, Lattica will not execute the trade until the price of the market is 40 cents.

Market orders are the default order type. Price trigger orders can be selected by clicking “Market” in the top right of the trade panel.
