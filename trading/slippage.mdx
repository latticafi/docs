---
title: "Slippage"
---

Slippage is the difference between a trade's expected price and its actual execution price. For example, you want to buy 5,000 YES shares. The best ask shows **$0.60**, but the order book looks like this:

| Ask price | Size available |
| :-------- | :------------- |
| $0.60     | 1,000          |
| $0.61     | 2,000          |
| $0.63     | 3,000          |

A market order for 5,000 shares executes as:

- 1,000 @ $0.60 = $600
- 2,000 @ $0.61 = $1,220
- 2,000 @ $0.63 = $1,260
- **Total: $3,080 → average fill $0.616**

The slippage is then:

- Expected cost at the quoted $0.60: $3,000
- Actual cost: $3,080
- Slippage: **$80, or about 2.7%** ($0.016 per share)

To protect against slippage, Lattica allows traders to adjust their max slippage tolerance. To do so, click the pencil icon next to “Slippage” in the bottom of the trade panel.
