---
title: "Take profit and stop loss orders (TP/SL)"
---

TP/SL orders close your position at certain selected market prices. TPs execute at prices above the market price at origination, SLs execute at prices below the market price at origination.

TP/SL orders can be dragged on the TradingView chart. Note that dragging in a way that causes the order to immediately execute will lead to an error. Usually this prevents user mistakes, but if this is your desired behavior you can manually close the order from the position table or order form.

By default, TP/SLs have a size equal to the entire position. In other words, if executed, your entire position is closed. If you instead wish to only TP/SL a specific percentage of the position, that is adjustable in the trade panel when you open a trade. Importantly, TP/SLs percentages are fixed. Once selected, they can not be changed mid-trade.
